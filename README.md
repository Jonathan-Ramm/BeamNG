## Mod Details

### FSAE FT 25
![FT25 Pic](/FormulaS/vehicles/ft25/default.jpg)

**Type:** Vehicle
**Description:**  
A unique fast Formula Student Car made based on the FT25 by FaSTTUBe TU-Berlin. It features 2 motor RWD and 4 motor AWD Configs. 
It has a total of 20k Downloads at the current time and is available at the [Repository](https://www.beamng.com/resources/fsae-ft25.37891/)
